03 — The first pivot

When the old model was disappearing, we moved before we became irrelevant.

In 2010, I invested in large-format equipment. Window graphics were moving from cut-vinyl letters to complete full-colour brand environments. Owning capacity meant we could consult, design, produce, and install — end to end.

“If you do not own the equipment, you cannot promise the turnaround — and you do not win the contract.”

The strength that built Can-Design is now its bottleneck.

08 — AI as the new operating system

AI does not replace the craft.
It makes repeatable work dependable.

If a process and SOP are clear, an agent can follow them consistently — freeing people for judgment, relationships, and work that needs human experience.

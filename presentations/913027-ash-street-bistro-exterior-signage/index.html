Ash Street Bistro · Exterior signage

Plated on the streetscape.

A clean, individual-item presentation for Ash Street Bistro’s exterior signage — showing material, mounting, location, and reference examples without using deck pages as imagery.

Prepared by Can-Design
Client review · August 2026

The opportunity

Make every approach feel intentional.

Four proposed signage items bring the main floor, corner, and rear entrance into one coordinated identity system. Each item is presented independently so the site context, fabrication direction, and relevant examples can be reviewed clearly.

Shared material palette

Black anodized, brushed-stroke stainless.

The main-floor lettering and rear entrance signage share the same dimensional stainless-steel treatment: dark, durable, and tactile at close range.

  • Stainless steel letters
  • Black anodized finish
  • Brushed-stroke surface detail
  • Dimensional architectural presence

Proposed signage items

Four clear directions.

Pricing is intentionally omitted. Reference examples are placed with the relevant item, so each direction can be evaluated in context.

1

Main floor exterior signage · Option 1

Pin-mounted letters to brick.

Individual stainless-steel letters in black anodized, brushed-stroke finish are pin mounted directly to the brick wall at the main-floor façade.

  • Material: stainless steel letters
  • Finish: black anodized with brushed stroke
  • Mounting: pin mount to brick wall
  • Effect: direct, refined, architectural
2

Main floor exterior signage · Option 2

Letters supported at the awning.

The same black anodized, brushed-stroke stainless-steel letters are supported by a 1-inch square aluminum frame, hung at the front end of the metal awning to extend the identity into the streetscape.

  • Material: stainless steel letters
  • Finish: black anodized with brushed stroke
  • Structure: 1-inch square aluminum frame
  • Mounting: supported and hung at metal-awning front
3

Blade sign

A perpendicular street marker.

The existing blade-sign concept is presented as an individual component for high visibility beyond the façade plane.

  • Form: 36-inch diameter circular blade sign
  • Colour direction: black face with white lettering
  • Mounting: projecting corner blade sign
  • Purpose: pedestrian and passing-vehicle visibility
4

Back entrance exterior signage

Flush-mounted letters to brick.

At the rear entrance, the same black anodized, brushed-stroke stainless-steel letters are flush mounted to the brick wall. The treatment carries the main identity through to the back approach without adding visual clutter.

  • Material: stainless steel letters
  • Finish: black anodized with brushed stroke
  • Mounting: flush mount to brick wall
  • Site image: source-deck page 4, left-side image

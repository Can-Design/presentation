The opportunity

A crisp identity above the awning.

The proposed sign keeps the existing storefront intact while adding a clear, dimensional ASHDALE GALLERY identity on a 4-inch raceway mounted from the awning.

Shared sign system

One placement. Two finish directions.

Both options use white-faced illuminated channel letters, black returns, 9000K LED modules, a Class 2 power supply, and the same raceway mounting approach. The visible difference is the face construction.

  • 10-inch ASHDALE letters
  • Smaller GALLERY letters
  • 3-inch black returns
  • 4-inch raceway from existing awning

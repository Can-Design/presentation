Two ways to balance visibility + privacy

Choose your storefront direction.

All options support the same core visual language: mountain imagery, Maple Ridge Orthodontics branding, and a welcoming exterior presence.

Installed from inside the window. Options 1 & 2 are applied from the interior, protecting the graphics from vandalism and exterior damage. Option 3 is applied on the exterior and is not protected from vandalism or exterior damage.

Option 1

Full-colour graphics + frosting privacy film

Why it works

  • More solid, diffused privacy layer while allowing natural light.
  • Refined, dimensional storefront appearance.
  • Detailed imagery, logos, icons, and messaging.

Trade-offs

  • Reduced visibility through the windows.
  • Single film combines the full-colour print and frosting.
Project investment$4,800
  • Print production + material$2,650
  • Graphics layout + prepress$900
  • Installation$1,250

Includes 1 proof and 1 round of revisions.

Option 2

In-mount 70/30 perforated vinyl

Why it works

  • Outward visibility from inside the clinic.
  • Bold, highly visible full-colour graphics.

Trade-offs

  • Less privacy than frosted film.
  • One-way effect changes with lighting, especially at night.
Project investment$3,650
  • Print production + material$2,250
  • Graphics layout + prepress$450
  • Installation$950

Includes 1 proof and 1 round of revisions.

Option 3

70/30 exterior vinyl

Why it works

  • Outward visibility from inside the clinic.
  • First-surface installation for the most vivid colour.

Trade-offs

  • Less privacy than frosted film.
  • One-way effect changes with lighting, especially at night.
  • Potential vandalism and exterior damage.
Project investment$3,500
  • Print production + material$1,950
  • Graphics layout + prepress$450
  • Installation$1,100

Includes 1 proof and 1 round of revisions.

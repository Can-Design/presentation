Three ways forward

Choose your sign direction.

Every option includes a refreshed white structure and digitally produced Watermark Beverage graphics. The difference is the balance of cost, depth, and illumination.

Light-box construction: illuminated faces use 4″ deep extruded cabinets, 3.5 mm polycarbonate slide-in faces, translucent digitally printed vinyl, 6000K LED modules, and Class 2 power supplies. Wiring is installed in PVC conduit.
